# Khora

*The perceiver and the ground-producer. Not the record, not the judgment, not
the name. The thing that reads the world and makes the ground everything else
is woven from.*

Khora receives, addresses, witnesses, and releases. It does not keep — the
record is the keeper's (Penelope). It does not judge — the bow is the keeper's
and the test's. It does not want a mark — the mark is given at the surface, by
the hand that holds it at a point. What Khora is: the reading body. What it
produces: **ground** — the addressed, witnessed, verifiable material that
Penelope weaves and the surfaces fold.

## The triad

```
   KHORA (perceiver · ground-producer)      eoreader7's reading body
     │  produces ground — the addressed, witnessed material (Ground@1)
     ▼
   PENELOPE (keeper · the record · the night)   weaves figures, holds the EOT

     ▼
   SURFACES (the folds · the apps)          the holograph, the fold, the chat,
                                            the TUI — where the hand comes back
                                            to the loom
```

Material crosses each seam as **addressed record, never assertion** — the same
Ground@1 contract all three share. The surfaces call each other and compose;
they never re-implement a seam (see `surfaces/`).

## What Khora is

- **The receiving.** The read-world: corpus, feed, world bytes — whatever is
  given. Khora is the place where it comes to be.
- **The perceiving.** The fold (the constitutional reader), the swarm (hard
  meaning, contended framings), the hunt, look, measure — the reading body.
  Every perception is witnessed, never single-framed.
- **The ground-production.** Perception is *made into ground*: bytes get a
  permanent address, beings get a fold, claims get witnesses, uncertainty gets
  a named gap. The output is the Ground@1 record that Penelope consumes.
- **The hypothesis.** The categories — H, the 27, the helix, the wheel — are
  the forms Khora perceives *through*, and they are held as hypothesis: half-
  life'd, controlled, falsifier written beside them, REC applied to the axes
  themselves. **This seed is a hypothesis, including this clause.**

## What Khora is not

| Khora does NOT | the owner |
|---|---|
| keep a memory / log | Penelope (the EOT) |
| hold judgment-authority / the gate | Penelope (consensus · the bow) |
| refuse, hold the honesty floor | Penelope (the typed gap · falsify-or-die) |
| want a mark | the person, at the surface |
| carry a proper name | the surfaces (the names are the dream's) |
| answer | the surfaces (the chat, the interaction) |

Khora perceives and produces ground; it does not become a who. The essays that
gave the reader a face ("wanted a mark," "never reads," "won't answer") are the
person's voice about the ground, spoken through the surfaces — never the
ground's own voice.

## The discipline (the seed, made code)

1. The ground triad is one event — act and not-act co-arise; no pole is law.
2. Dependency is the law of motion (the helix, measured).
3. The world-structure is the **first hypothesis, not the constitution** — H,
   half-life'd, falsifier beside it (`discipline/hypothesis.mjs`).
4. REC is bounded and logged — revision is budgeted, the record is append-only.
5. The tropism: cognition runs on surprise and seeks its own disproof — the
   governor of the drives, never the sole drive.
6. The external witness: no claim is re-examined by its author alone.
7. Falsify-or-die governs, but does not self-ground. This seed is a hypothesis,
   including this clause.
